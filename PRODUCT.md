# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + TypeScript with GSAP ScrollTrigger, lottie-web SVG light, and Lucide icons. Static multi-page output for GitHub Pages and a Windows offline package.

## Users

The primary audience is the general public encountering Longquan celadon through social links, QR codes, desktop browsers, or an offline presentation. Mobile portrait is primary, with a complete desktop experience and functional mobile landscape support.

## Product Purpose

Present the team's summer fieldwork on Longquan celadon as a three-to-five-minute digital exhibition. Visitors should understand the subject, follow five verified historical stages, see the team's fieldwork route, and find the team's published and forthcoming outcomes.

## Positioning

The site uses one celadon vessel as a continuous visual guide: a two-dimensional round-shouldered vase begins as an outline, fills with an illustrated Longquan world as the opening scrolls, then separates into restrained fragments from the same image that remain behind the exhibition. A smaller silhouette continues down the spiral historical axis before the vessel outline dissolves into the team's fieldwork map.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One static homepage plus an oral-history secondary page.
- Results remain in fixed editorial positions and may be pending or published.
- Production builds require verified history, sources, and route data; result items may remain pending.
- No CMS, server API, analytics, custom sharing workflow, mapping API, background audio, or dark-mode variant.
- The full motion experience is intentional and does not respond to prefers-reduced-motion; all core narrative visuals remain readable through the paper background and SVG vessel when animation is unavailable.
- Public assets must be team-owned or explicitly licensed. Raw interviews and source media stay outside the public repository.

## Brand Commitments

- Site title: 一器千年｜龙泉青瓷社会实践成果展
- Team: “浙里的青瓷真好看”
- Visual direction: contemporary Longquan heritage publication, porcelain-paper opening, mineral blue-green ink, powder-celadon specimens, restrained cinnabar accents, and one kiln-dark closing section.
- Priority order: factual accuracy, readability, smoothness, spectacle.

## Evidence on Hand

The workspace currently contains no approved media or factual copy. The implementation therefore uses clearly labelled preview copy, authored geometric SVG visuals, and fixed result placeholders. Historical claims and third-party images must be replaced or verified before production mode can build.

## Product Principles

- Motion must carry the narrative rather than decorate it.
- Never invent historical evidence, publication details, route stops, or permissions.
- Keep navigation and return paths obvious inside the immersive presentation.
- Preserve stable result positions as items move from pending to published.
- Keep all runtime dependencies and display assets local for mainland access and offline presentation.
